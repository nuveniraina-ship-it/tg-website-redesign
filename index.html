    Q.01What is Ticket Generator?

    Ticket Generator is an online platform that allows event organizers to easily create, customize, distribute, and validate event tickets with secure QR Code verification. It also streamlines event management by facilitating registration, payment processing, real-time attendance tracking, and detailed post-event analytics, making it ideal for events of any scale.

    Q.02I don't know how many people will show up directly at the event. How can I use Ticket Generator for events?

    Create an event page and let guests register — for free or with paid tickets. Each registration gets a ticket automatically, so your guest list builds itself, and live insights show registrations and check-ins as they happen. With pay-as-you-go pricing, you only pay for the tickets you actually issue.

    Q.03I have a ticket design. How can Ticket Generator help?

    Upload your own ticket design and Ticket Generator adds a unique QR Code to every ticket. You can then send the tickets via email, SMS, or WhatsApp, or export them as printable files — and validate each one at entry with the Ticket Validator app.

    Q.04I don't even have a ticket design. How can Ticket Generator help?

    No designer needed. Pick a ready-made template and customize it with a background image or color, your logo, and basic event details. Your branded tickets are ready to send in minutes.

    Q.05I already have an events app to get registrations and payments. How can I use Ticket Generator?

    Keep your existing app and connect it to the Ticket Generator API. Tickets are generated and distributed in real time as registrations come in, and you can still validate entries and track attendance with Ticket Generator.

    Q.06If I have the guest list, can I directly send tickets without any event page or registrations?

    Yes. With Direct Ticketing, you can send ready-made or customized tickets automatically to everyone on your guest list — no event page or registration form required.
