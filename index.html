    Have a Question?

    Frequently asked questions

    Get answers to commonly asked questions about QR Code Ticket Generator

    Q.01What is Ticket Generator?

    Ticket Generator is an online platform that lets event organizers create, sell, distribute, and validate event tickets with secure and verified QR Code. Manage registrations, payments, attendance, and analytics — without any commissions or subscriptions with online Ticket Generator.

    Q.02I am organizing an event. What can I use Ticket Generator for?

    Whether you are organizing an event for 20 people or 10,000 people, you can sell event tickets directly without paying any commission. Ticket Generator is an easy-to-use online tool to create an event page, get registrations/payments, approve registrations, design tickets, download/send tickets, validate tickets at entry, get attendance data, and more. You can create standard tickets, reserved seating tickets, luxury tickets, and other custom ticket categories for single-day and multi-day events. Collect varying prices for each ticket category. Compared to standard ticketing platforms, Ticket Generator is highly affordable and helps you increase event margins.

    Q.03I don't know how many people will show up directly at the event. How can I use Ticket Generator for events?

    You can use e-ticket generator to generate and download ticket images/PDFs. You can print these tickets and sell them directly at the box office. Alternatively, you can use the web application in real-time to generate and send the tickets one-by-one at the box office.

    Q.04How do ticket add-ons work?

    Add-ons can be:

    Bundled – Included as part of the main ticket package.
    Optional – Selected during purchase for an additional payment.

    Organizers can configure which add-ons are available and how they are priced. The same ticket can be scanned at different checkpoints (e.g., entry gate, parking area, lunch counter) via Ticket Validator to verify the services included.

    Q.05I have a ticket design. How can Ticket Generator help?

    You can upload your own ticket design (created using Canva, Adobe Spark, or any other tool). Ticket Generator will help you add a unique QR Code, ticket code, and other information (guest name, hall number, seat number, and more). Event coordinators can validate these tickets at the entry using Ticket Validator mobile app.

    Q.06I don't even have a ticket design. How can Ticket Generator help?

    Use Ticket Generator's standard template to quickly generate tickets. Simply provide event details (name, description, venue, date, and timings), and branding (logo, background color/image). You can also add unique ticket information such as guest name, hall number, seat number, and more as per your needs. Each ticket will have a QR Code that can be validated at the entry using Ticket Validator.

    Q.07I already have an events app to get registrations and payments. How can I use Ticket Generator?

    If you have an event registration website that allows attendees to register for your event and purchase tickets, you can use Ticket Generator API. It will help you programmatically generate secure tickets in real-time. As soon as ticket purchase is successful, make a POST call to Ticket Generator API and get a QR Code ticket that can be shown in the app or sent directly to the attendee.

    Q.08If I have the guest list, can I directly send tickets without any event page or registrations?

    Yes. With Direct Ticketing, you can send ready-made or customized tickets automatically to everyone on your guest list — no event page or registration form required.
