Q.01What are ticket credits?

In Ticket Generator, ticket credits are required to generate tickets. Generating one ticket requires one ticket credit. First 10 ticket credits are free and added to the account on sign up.

Q.02How many ticket credits do I need?

One credit = One ticket. Therefore, your account should have enough credits to generate the number of tickets you need. Depending on the tickets you need, choose a credit package. The package options allow you to buy credits in multiples of 10—10, 100, 1,000, and 10,000.

Q.03Can ticket credits be refunded?

No, ticket credits once purchased cannot be refunded. However, credits don't expire and can be used to generate tickets anytime.

Q.04What is the cost of creating a seating plan?

Creating one seating plan costs 100 USD or 200 credits. This includes the design and setup of a complete seating layout by the Ticket Generator team. Once the layout is created, you can review and approve it, or request changes before it is finalized and used for your events.

Q.05How can I buy more credits?

To purchase ticket credits, go to Billing in the Ticket Generator application and choose the required credit package.
